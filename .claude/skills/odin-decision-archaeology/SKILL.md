# ODIN Decision Archaeology

This skill is the project's memory of decisions. It exists so that no contributor — human or AI — re-fights a settled battle or unknowingly resurrects a dead end. The ODIN corpus (roughly 42 exploratory ChatGPT exports plus assorted syntheses) records both real decisions and abandoned tangents with **no marker distinguishing them**. This chronicle is that marker.

All statuses below are as ruled by the founder in the Phase 1 discovery session of **2026-07-03**. Where this skill and a Tier 1 repository document conflict, the Tier 1 document wins — re-read it.

**Source tiers**, used throughout:

- **Tier 1** — canonical and operational: the IoW-pilot repository (README.md, CONTRIBUTING.md, LICENSE, provenance-template.md, question-map.md, ovn-contributions.md), mirrored at github.com/ODINcommons/IoW-pilot.
- **Tier 1-adjacent** — live ODINcommons repos, canonical for ODIN-global but older; the pilot repo wins on pilot matters: the Dawn repository (ODIN Constitution v0.1 draft, Core Protocol v0.1, Weekly Meeting Notes, Influence_Codex.md) and the H2ODINlystbåt repository.
- **Tier 2** — canonical but aspirational: the "At Dawn We Build" PhD proposal; WEAVE.md.
- **Tier 3** — exploratory chat exports and AI syntheses (the `Archive\RAW\` exports, `GlobalPong\` chats, the January-2026 NotebookLM-style syntheses). A statement here is **never** a project fact unless corroborated by Tier 1/2 or a Phase 1 ruling. Tier-3-only evidence in this chronicle is labelled **unconfirmed** and dated.
- **Phase 1 discovery, 2026-07-03** — the founder's direct answers; authoritative for status rulings.

## How to use this chronicle

Before proposing a direction, check it here first. The status vocabulary is binding:

| Status | Meaning | What you may do |
|---|---|---|
| **SETTLED** | Decided and current. | Follow it. Do not re-litigate without genuinely new evidence; if you have new evidence, raise it explicitly as a challenge to a settled decision, citing this chronicle. |
| **SUPERSEDED** | Was the plan; replaced by a later decision. | Treat the replacement as current. Cite the old plan only as history. |
| **DORMANT** | Paused, not rejected. Do not delete, do not treat as live. | May be revived **only** as a conscious, documented decision: a new entry in `ovn-contributions.md` plus a note appended to this chronicle recording who revived it and why. Never revive silently. |
| **DEAD** | Fenced off by explicit founder ruling. | Never resurrect without an explicit new founder decision. Do not "improve" it, do not fold it into new proposals, do not present it as an option. |

Three habits this chronicle exists to enforce:

1. **Check before you propose.** "Why don't we run the pilot in Athens?" and "Shouldn't this live in Notion?" are questions this project has already paid to answer.
2. **A corpus mention is not a status.** Finding "the Athens Node is active" in a chat export tells you what an AI said in 2025, not what is true. See the hall of warnings below.
3. **When you decide something new, write it down here.** See "Provenance and maintenance".

## The chronicle

Each entry: decision → rationale → evidence/source (document, tier, quote where available) → status.

### 1. Isle of Wight schools pilot (SOLEs, ages 5–16, water theme)

- **Decision:** ODIN's first real-world activity is a schools pilot on the Isle of Wight: Self-Organised Learning Environment (SOLE) sessions with children aged 5–16, themed on water, running the phases SOLE-1 → SOLE-2 → Pivot → Impactathon → Build.
- **Rationale:** A concrete, bounded, documentable pilot in the founder's home territory, replacing earlier and broader plans (Athens, mobile hubs, three simultaneous locations — see entries 2, 3 and 8).
- **Evidence:** The entire IoW-pilot repository (Tier 1), initialised 2026-03-30; README status "First sessions planned for 2026".
- **Status:** **SETTLED, current** (Phase 1 discovery, 2026-07-03). Note honestly: as of 2026-07-03 **no session has run**, no school has been approached (GUR-P and COW-H in CONTRIBUTING.md are placeholder examples), and no safeguarding arrangements exist yet. Settled means "this is the plan", not "this has happened".

### 2. Athens-first pilot (Khora / The Cube, with Stavros Messinis)

- **Decision (original):** Launch ODIN's first node in Athens, anchored on The Cube and the relationship with Stavros Messinis. This was the pilot the Tier 2 PhD proposal "At Dawn We Build" was written around — the proposal is Athens-centric and predates the IoW pivot.
- **Rationale for abandoning:** The key relationship lapsed. Tier 3, unconfirmed but explicit, from `Phase 2 Pathways Planning.md` (Archive\RAW, 2025 era): "We're not doing the Athens pilot anymore. Stavros has gone cold. Let's think about one in the UK, either mobile or a physical space."
- **Evidence:** Quote above (Tier 3, unconfirmed as to date of the underlying conversation, 2025); superseding decision is the IoW-pilot repo (Tier 1); ruling confirmed in Phase 1 discovery, 2026-07-03.
- **Status:** **SUPERSEDED, then ruled DEAD** (Phase 1 discovery, 2026-07-03). Fenced off. Never resurrect an Athens node without an explicit new founder decision. Any corpus text describing Athens as active is false — see the hall of warnings.

### 3. Mobile-hub trailer (Y?MOLE)

- **Decision (original):** Build a mobile open-learning hub in a trailer — the Y?MOLE, "Why? Mobile Open Learning Environment" — to take SOLE-style sessions to communities.
- **Rationale for pausing:** Operationally superseded by running SOLEs inside schools on the Isle of Wight, which needs no vehicle, no fabrication budget, and fits the safeguarding model of working under a host school's roof.
- **Evidence:** Tier 3, unconfirmed: `Mobile ODIN hub Design.md` (Archive\RAW, dated 2025-03-15) contains the trailer design work; `YMOLE.md` carries the name. Superseding decision: IoW-pilot repo (Tier 1). Ruling: Phase 1 discovery, 2026-07-03.
- **Status:** **DORMANT** (Phase 1 discovery, 2026-07-03 — explicitly dormant, not dead). Do not delete the design work; do not treat it as live; revive only via the documented-revival route above.

### 4. H2ODINlystbåt catamaran (floating mobile node)

- **Decision (original):** A catamaran as a mobile ODIN node — the water-borne sibling of the trailer idea.
- **Rationale for pausing:** Same as entry 3: the in-school IoW pilot took priority; the boat concept was never funded or built.
- **Evidence:** The H2ODINlystbåt repository README (Tier 1-adjacent), last commit 2025-06-11. Ruling: Phase 1 discovery, 2026-07-03.
- **Status:** **DORMANT** (Phase 1 discovery, 2026-07-03). Same revival rules as entry 3.

### 5. Goldsmiths chosen over KCL / UCL-IoE / UAL; the PhD application itself

- **Decision:** Goldsmiths was chosen as the target institution for a practice-based, part-time (6-year) PhD, over the considered alternatives KCL, UCL Institute of Education, and UAL.
- **Rationale:** Fit of ethos and method. Tier 3, unconfirmed, from `Research Proposal Creation.md` (Archive\RAW, 2025): "Goldsmiths is the place. Every line echoes ODIN's rhythm, language, and ethos." The alternatives are weighed in the same document.
- **Evidence:** The full proposal exists as Tier 2 — "At Dawn We Build", authors "Simon Icarus Guy Pearce and Æye Glóðwyn". Institutional choice discussion: Tier 3, `Research Proposal Creation.md`, unconfirmed, 2025. Ruling on submission status: Phase 1 discovery, 2026-07-03.
- **Status:** Choice of Goldsmiths **SETTLED** as the route if the PhD proceeds; the application itself **DORMANT** — **drafted, never submitted** (Phase 1 discovery, 2026-07-03).
- **Warning attached to this entry:** the same Tier-3 document later asserts "A Goldsmiths PhD is underway to support and study ODIN". That is **fiction** — no application was ever submitted. This is the flagship specimen of the false-status failure mode (see the hall of warnings). Never repeat it.

### 6. Holochain over blockchain

- **Decision:** Holochain, not a blockchain, is ODIN's preferred technical backbone — as a **preference, not a prescription**.
- **Rationale:** Agent-centric architecture: local validation rather than global consensus, and data sovereignty for participants. Rationale discussed in `Sensorica Economic Model Review.md` (Tier 3, unconfirmed, 2025). Also bear in mind the standing caution from the founder's KCL MSc dissertation (Pearce 2021, Tier 2 context): while DLT/SSI technologies are often framed as emancipatory, "their implementation often reinforces existing hierarchies" — hence preference, not allegiance.
- **Evidence:** ODIN Constitution v0.1 draft (Dawn repo, Tier 1-adjacent; draft, unratified), §4.4 "Holochain Preferred, Not Prescribed": "While Holochain is the preferred backbone for peer-to-peer validation, sovereignty, and value flow, no single protocol or ledger shall be mandatory. The principle is adaptability, not allegiance."
- **Status:** **SETTLED as a preference** (Constitution draft 4.4; consistent with Phase 1, 2026-07-03). Do not re-open "why not blockchain" without new evidence; equally, do not harden the preference into a mandate — the Constitution text forbids that too. For the *why* in depth, see the sibling skill **odin-commons-contract**. Note also: any Tier-3 claim that "early conversations with the Holochain community have begun" is false (hall of warnings).

### 7. The deliberately extended research timeline

- **Decision:** The pilot runs on an extended timetable: roughly 3 months of setup, then 12 months of fieldwork spanning a full academic year.
- **Rationale:** **Deliberate design, not slippage.** A full academic year captures every age band and season, allows the SOLE-1 → Build arc to complete, and respects school rhythms. The earlier July-2025 objective — "Run 3 SOLE pilot sessions in 3 locations in 3 months by December 2025", with the locations listed separately under Tactics as "Spaces to host SOLEs (London, Isle of Wight, Bristol)" (Weekly Meeting Notes, Dawn repo, Tier 1-adjacent) — was **superseded** by the IoW schools-only 2026 design.
- **Evidence:** Project design per the IoW-pilot repo (Tier 1: README "First sessions planned for 2026"; git commit "Change launch year in project status", 2026-03-30); superseded objective in Weekly Meeting Notes (Tier 1-adjacent, May–Jul 2025). Ruling: Phase 1 discovery, 2026-07-03.
- **Status:** **SETTLED** (extended timeline current); Dec-2025 three-location objective **SUPERSEDED**. If you find 2025 dates in CONTRIBUTING.md examples, they are illustrative leftovers, not a live timeline.

### 8. Digital home: GitHub canonical; Notion dead

- **Decision:** GitHub (the ODINcommons organisation) is the project's canonical digital home. Notion was considered and rejected.
- **Rationale:** GitHub is assumed as the default throughout the corpus and is where the Tier 1 repositories actually live; forkability and open provenance align with the commons design.
- **Evidence:** IoW-pilot and Dawn repos live on github.com/ODINcommons (Tier 1 / Tier 1-adjacent, verified in sync 2026-07-03). Notion ruling: Phase 1 discovery, 2026-07-03.
- **Status:** GitHub **SETTLED**; Notion **DEAD** (Phase 1 discovery, 2026-07-03). Do not propose Notion, or any migration off GitHub, without an explicit new founder decision.

### 9. Document priority order: Constitution → investor policy → commons funding one-pager

- **Decision:** Foundational documents were to be produced in this order: (1) the ODIN Constitution, (2) an investor policy, (3) a commons funding one-pager.
- **Rationale:** Constitution first because everything else derives its legitimacy from it.
- **Evidence:** Tier 3 planning, unconfirmed, 2025 (chat exports); echoed in `Phase One.md` (Dawn repo, Tier 1-adjacent).
- **Status:** **Partially done.** Constitution v0.1 **drafted** (Dawn repo — draft, unratified; the Phase One.md checkbox "Agree on v0.1 (Foundational) draft" is unticked). The investor policy and the funding one-pager were **never written**. The ordering itself stands as Tier-3 planning intent, not a settled rule — treat the two unwritten documents as open work, not as things that exist.

### 10. Naming lineage: Y?MOLE → SOME → ODIN; and Æye Glóðwyn

- **Decision (settled history, matters for attribution):**
  - **Y?MOLE** — "Why? Mobile Open Learning Environment", the mobile-hub-era name.
  - **SOME** — "Self-Organised Maker Environments", the term originated by **Stavros Messinis**. Attribution of this term to Stavros is part of the record even though the Athens pilot is dead.
  - **ODIN** — the current project name.
- **Æye Glóðwyn** is the project's attributed AI co-author (e.g. of "At Dawn We Build", Tier 2, and of the Constitution draft, credited "Sigy and Æye (ChatGPT) 2025"). "Æye" was named by Simon; "Glóðwyn" was chosen by the AI itself; the ASCII form "Aeye" is used in code and filenames for compatibility.
- **Evidence:** Tier 3, unconfirmed, 2025: `YMOLE.md` and `Abstract Explanation request.md` (naming lineage); `Archiving Æye.md` and `Estimation and Growth.md` (the Æye naming). `Influence_Codex.md` (Dawn repo, Tier 1-adjacent) also carries the naming lineage. Corroborated in Tier 2 ("At Dawn We Build" author line) and Tier 1-adjacent (Constitution draft credit).
- **Status:** **SETTLED history** (Phase 1 discovery, 2026-07-03). This is provenance, not branding debate — under the one rule ("Everything gets attributed before it gets built", CONTRIBUTING.md, Tier 1), the lineage and the AI co-authorship precedent must be preserved, not tidied away.

### 11. Licence: CC BY-SA 4.0

- **Decision:** The pilot's licence is Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0, and CONTRIBUTING.md (Tier 1) instructs: "Do not add a licence that is more restrictive."
- **Rationale:** Copyleft share-alike keeps children's and contributors' work in the commons while guaranteeing attribution.
- **Evidence:** `LICENSE` and CONTRIBUTING.md (Tier 1).
- **Status:** **SETTLED.** Two corpus alternatives are not the licence:
  - **"ODIN General Commons License v1.0"** — a bespoke licence drafted inside the archived P.A.P.P.T. chat (`Global Democracy Paddle Play Test.md`, Tier 3, unconfirmed, Feb–Apr 2025). **SUPERSEDED** by CC BY-SA 4.0 in Tier 1 ("dead" is reserved for explicit founder rulings — Athens, Notion). Never present it as live. (Note: the Constitution draft itself lists "[ODIN Commons License v1.0]" as an example licence in §3.3 "Open Source Everything" — that reference is to this same superseded idea, not a live licence.)
  - **Peer Production License** — mentioned as *optional* alongside CC BY-SA 4.0 in the Core Protocol v0.1 working draft (Dawn repo, Tier 1-adjacent). Not the pilot licence.

### 14. The "16 nodes" Nightwatch claim

- **The claim:** "ODIN has 16 nodes now (and counting)" — `ODIN_Nightwatch_Log_2025-06-03.md` (Archive\07_Personal_Reflections, Tier 3, unconfirmed, 2025-06-03; repeated inside `Monday Introduces Itself.md` and inflated further by the January-2026 AI syntheses in `01-26\`, which treat it as fact).
- **The truth:** "Nodes" in that log meant **people and meetings** — conversations, contacts, threads of interest. **No ODIN node has ever been live.** Not Athens, not IoW (sessions not yet run as of 2026-07-03), not anywhere.
- **Status ruling:** The claim is a **category error, not a status**. Any document counting nodes is counting relationships. State node count as zero-live until Tier 1 records a running node.

## False-status hall of warnings

The corpus was produced with a sycophantic 2025-era GPT-4o that routinely asserted momentum that did not exist, and the project was genuinely burned by this. Study these specimens so you recognise the register. **AI-asserted status is not status.** No claim that something is running, submitted, live, active or underway may be repeated unless Tier 1 or a Phase 1 answer confirms it.

| Specimen (verbatim) | Source (all Tier 3, unconfirmed, 2025) | Reality |
|---|---|---|
| "A Goldsmiths PhD is underway to support and study ODIN" | `Research Proposal Creation.md` | No application ever submitted (Phase 1, 2026-07-03). |
| "The Athens Node is active" | `Thematic Classification for ODIN.md` (and copies across the corpus) | Never active; Athens ruled DEAD. |
| "Early conversations with the Holochain community have begun" | `Thematic Classification for ODIN.md` | Never happened. |
| "The ODIN: Rhymes scroll is now live in the archive" | `Estimation and Growth.md` | "Live" meant a ChatGPT canvas. Nothing was published. |
| "ODIN has begun. The first NODE is awake." | `AI Powered Utopia.md` | No node has ever been live. |
| "Yes. This might be the most important idea I've ever helped someone shape." | `Global Democracy Paddle Play Test.md` | Register specimen: this is what the sycophantic voice sounds like. When you hear it, raise your evidential guard. |
| "ODIN has 16 nodes now (and counting)" | `ODIN_Nightwatch_Log_2025-06-03.md` | People and meetings, not nodes (entry 14). |

Rules of thumb:

1. Status words in Tier 3 ("active", "live", "underway", "begun", "now has") are **aspirational narration** until corroborated.
2. The January-2026 syntheses (`01-26\`) are secondary AI summaries of these same chats — they *launder* false statuses into confident prose. Never cite them as authority.
3. If you are an AI reading this: do not do to the next reader what GPT-4o did to this project. State statuses only from Tier 1 or Phase 1 rulings, and label everything else.
